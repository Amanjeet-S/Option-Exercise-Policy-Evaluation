# Development and contributions

Read the [development guide](docs/development.md), [research roadmap](docs/roadmap.md) and [source record](PROVENANCE.md) before proposing a change.

Explain the research question or defect addressed, state the assumptions affected, and include the smallest reproducible example that supports the change. Distinguish implementation corrections from changes to the research design. Update documentation when conventions or results change.

Run the automated checks and execute changed notebooks from a fresh kernel. Numerical changes require suitable analytical benchmarks or independent checks. Data changes require source URLs, retrieval dates, units, transformation records and reuse conditions.

Keep empirical observations separate from simulated validation inputs. Do not select specifications solely because they produce a preferred sign or significance level. Record unsuccessful checks and material limitations. Credit ideas, datasets and adapted code at the point of use.

Keep credentials, account exports, private correspondence and restricted provider files outside the tracked repository. Discuss substantive new research directions before treating them as part of the established design.

## Licensing contributions

Original software contributions are offered under [MIT](LICENSE), and original research-writing contributions under [CC BY 4.0](LICENSES/CC-BY-4.0.txt), unless separately agreed and clearly documented. Contributors retain their copyright. Follow the [licensing scope](LICENSING.md), identify third-party material and its terms, and submit only material that you have the right to contribute. Licensing a contribution does not clear restricted inputs or dependent findings for publication.
