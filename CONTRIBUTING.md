# Contributions

I license released original software under [MIT](LICENSE) and research writing under [CC BY 4.0](LICENSES/CC-BY-4.0.txt). Contributors retain their copyright and identify third-party material and its terms. See [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md).

Explain the research question or defect, affected assumptions and evidence supporting the change. Follow the [development checks](docs/development.md), use British English and no em dashes, and attribute established methods beside the relevant claims. Preserve unfavourable findings and frozen evidence. Claims of novelty, predictive ability or economic significance require evidence.

Keep credentials and restricted observations outside Git. Reviewed derived findings may be published under the confirmed permission; raw exports, cleaned observations and reversible transformations remain excluded under the [publication policy](docs/publication_policy.md).
