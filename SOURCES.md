# Sources and methodological attribution

The code, documentation and numerical test configuration were prepared for this repository. Standard financial models, theorems and algorithms retain the attribution below. No external implementation, paper table or figure has been incorporated into the project. Observed Treasury data are retained separately with source metadata.

The project implements the financial algorithms and experimental workflow. It uses library implementations for these numerical primitives. Dependency versions are recorded in the environment lock and each benchmark manifest. Upstream packages retain their own licences; their source files are not vendored here.

## Data and figures

Paths in the numerical validation record are simulated from a stated model using recorded pseudorandom seeds. Those test inputs are not calibrated market estimates. Validation figures and tables are generated by the experiment runner. The externally linked papers remain on their publishers' or academic hosts' websites.

Observed rates come from the [US Treasury's documented feed](https://home.treasury.gov/treasury-daily-interest-rate-xml-feed). Each saved snapshot includes the original XML, parsed observations, observation cutoff, retrieval time and source hash. See [the data plan](data_plan.md) for the distinction between published par yields and the eventual pricing curve, and for the remaining option-quote and underlying-data requirements.

The [Cboe Option EOD Summary product page](https://datashop.cboe.com/option-eod-summary), its [file specification](https://datashop.cboe.com/documents/Option_EOD_Summary_Layout.pdf) and its [published sample archive](https://datashop.cboe.com/download/sample/217) support the [format audit](cboe_sample_audit.md). The sample is provider demonstration material dated 25 August 2023, not the project's recent empirical dataset. Its raw files and the specification are stored privately with hashes. Provider field labels are reproduced where necessary to identify columns exactly; explanatory text is original.

## Scope of the contribution

The current contribution is a preliminary implementation, explicit exercise conventions, independent policy evaluation, validation checks and an observed-data acquisition record. The full research contribution remains to be established under the [staged design](research_design.md). Subsequent research conclusions must follow from saved experiments. Any future use of external code, data, prose or figures must add precise attribution and the applicable reuse terms before inclusion.
