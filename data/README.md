# Data

The [public Treasury snapshot](public/treasury/README.md) preserves its source, units, retrieval metadata and original bytes. It is a retrieval vintage and does not reconstruct the information available at a historical valuation cutoff.

`private/` is ignored by Git, but private storage is not permission for indefinite retention. LSEG observations and restricted transformed inputs are for the authorised individual research use; the university guidance requires deletion of underlying stored data when its project ends. The retention review includes duplicate exports, archives, private history compilations, caches and backups. Original independent simulations, permitted findings, code and source-free provenance require separate classification rather than deletion merely because they are stored here. Provider demonstrations have their own terms.

Public reproduction uses stipulated mathematical inputs and permitted research summaries. Exact historical replay requires lawful possession and permitted retention of the matching archive described in the [licensed-input guide](../replication/licensed_reproduction.md). I do not distribute that archive. If required inputs are deleted, exact historical replay is no longer available from this checkout; original hashes and findings are not rewritten to conceal that limitation.

The [publication policy](../docs/publication_policy.md) and [third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) distinguish released findings from excluded observations.
