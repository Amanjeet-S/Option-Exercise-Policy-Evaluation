# US Treasury par-yield snapshots

Source: [US Department of the Treasury, Daily Treasury Par Yield Curve Rates](https://home.treasury.gov/treasury-daily-interest-rate-xml-feed).

The `2026-09-14/` snapshot includes original XML, parsed CSV observations and a metadata record. It contains 175 observations from 2 January to 11 September 2026. Missing values remain missing.

Par yields are not zero-coupon rates or an overnight-indexed-swap discount curve. The saved observations do not establish a pricing curve without further modelling. Reproduction instructions are in the [Project 1 overview](../../../projects/01_optimal_stopping/README.md).
