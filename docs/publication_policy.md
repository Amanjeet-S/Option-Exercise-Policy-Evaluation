# Publication policy

## 1. Research findings and source observations

Permission to publish research findings derived from the project's LSEG access was confirmed on 27 September 2026. I may therefore release original estimates, numerical comparisons, figures and written conclusions after checking their contents. Raw subscription observations remain excluded. The confirmation concerns derived research findings; it does not turn a transformed dataset into an unrestricted source of market observations.

| Material | Repository treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Raw provider exports, quote histories and saved responses | Retain privately |
| Cleaned subsets, rounded quotes, quote midpoints, spreads, rescaled series and reversibly encoded observations | Retain privately |
| Model estimates, policy-value comparisons, statistical summaries, numerical bounds and research figures | Eligible for publication after checking that the released material does not disclose or permit reconstruction of restricted observations |
| Original methods, source code and explicitly stipulated mathematical examples | Eligible for publication after checking attribution, embedded data and third-party material |
| Open datasets already included with documented reuse permissions | Retain their existing source terms and attribution requirements |

## 2. Review of a release

I review the files together. A chart may be unobjectionable alone while a companion table supplies enough information to recover the underlying observations. The review therefore includes machine-readable supplements, notebook outputs, figures, PDF attachments and the history included in a release. The absence of raw CSV files is not sufficient if the same values are printed elsewhere.

A publication version may omit exact quotes, input curves, source identifiers or model-input bundles while reporting the resulting research findings. It must state what is available, what remains private and what access is required for full empirical reproduction. Private source records and earlier experiment manifests remain preserved; their historical publication flags are not rewritten to imply that permission existed earlier.
