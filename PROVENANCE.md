# Sources and provenance

I distinguish established mathematics, original implementation, stipulated analytical examples and model-conditional numerical findings. The [source register](SOURCES.md), [paper bibliography](paper/main.tex) and [literature comparison](paper/contribution_review.md) attribute the methods and delimit the contribution.

The [Treasury snapshot](data/public/treasury/README.md) preserves the original feed, retrieval metadata, units and checksums. Its par yields are not a verified pricing discount curve. The attributed event calendar records its source and historical scope. Subscription observations and restricted model-input bundles are held only for authorised research use and are subject to the completion-stage deletion requirement. I review copied archives and history compilations as well as working files. Source-free provenance and permitted findings can document a completed study without retaining its observations.

The [current inventory](replication/current_source_manifest.json) checks the selected reader sources. The [historical inventory](replication/source_manifest.json) belongs to the retained `v1.0.0` release. The [historical replay record](replication/historical_replay.md) concerns the original experiments and archive versions. Updating the current inventory does not change a registered experiment's source identities. Public examples do not establish market validity.

The [publication policy](docs/publication_policy.md) and [third-party notices](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) govern disclosure and attribution. Established results and independently written derivations are identified in the paper; I make no claim of theoretical priority.
